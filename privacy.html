Privacy policy

mcp-sheets is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads and writes the owner's own Google Sheets and Google Drive files. It does not access anyone else's data.

How the data is used

The data is used only by the owner's own scripts, on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own automated jobs. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
